---
name: Venue covers and visual simplicity
description: Preserve the full cover artwork and simplify pub/brewery detail pages without hiding actions.
---

Le copertine devono adattarsi al contenitore mantenendo le proporzioni, senza zoom, deformazioni o ritagli. Le schede devono essere più moderne, semplici e ordinate.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested proportionate, non-zoomed covers and less visual clutter, using venue-detail screenshots as references.

**How to apply:** show full pub/brewery cover artwork rather than cropping it to fill the frame; neutral space around unusual aspect ratios is preferable to losing image content. Keep the identity/logo separate rather than overlapping the cover. Use accessible disclosure for secondary information while preserving existing actions and content. This is a cover rule, not a ban on cropping every photo thumbnail.
